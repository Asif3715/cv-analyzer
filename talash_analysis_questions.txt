- Education
- Is the candidate’s early academic performance (SSE/HSSC) stable across stages?
- What are the candidate’s UG/PG marks or CGPA, and how do they compare after normalization?
- What is the actual degree progression path (e.g., BSc->MSc->MS/MPhil vs BS->MS->PhD)?
- Are degree titles, specializations, institutions, and timelines complete and consistent?
- What is the quality/ranking of attended institutions (THE/QS), and where is ranking unavailable?
- Does the profile show coherent educational progression and specialization consistency?
- Are there educational gaps between stages, and what are their durations?
- Are educational gaps justified by productive professional activities?
- Overall: how strong is the candidate’s educational profile?

- Research: Journals
- Is each journal venue legitimate and identifiable (e.g., ISSN-backed)?
- Is the journal indexed in Web of Science? If yes, what is the Impact Factor?
- Is the journal indexed in Scopus?
- What is the journal quartile (Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4)?
- What is the candidate’s authorship role (first/corresponding/both/other)?
- Overall: is each journal paper high-quality, moderate, or low/unverified?
- Are claimed rankings/quality verified from external sources rather than CV claims?

- Research: Conferences
- What is the conference’s recognized rank/standing (including A* if available)?
- Is the conference mature/established (series continuity)?
- Is the proceedings venue indexed/published on credible platforms (Scopus/IEEE/Springer/ACM)?
- What is the candidate’s authorship role in conference papers?
- Overall: how strong is each conference publication venue and contribution?

- Supervision
- How many MS/PhD students were supervised as main supervisor?
- How many MS/PhD students were supervised as co-supervisor?
- How many publications were produced with supervised students?
- Does the candidate actively publish with supervised students?
- Does the candidate appear as corresponding author (leadership signal)?
- Is the candidate listed before/after students (supervisory authorship convention)?
- How strong is the candidate’s contribution to student research productivity?

- Books
- What books are authored/co-authored by the candidate?
- Are metadata complete and verifiable (name, authors, ISBN, publisher, year, link)?
- What is the candidate’s authorship role (sole/lead/co-author/contributor)?
- How credible is the publisher and scholarly value of the book output?
- Are books accessible/verifiable via online sources?

- Patents
- Has the candidate produced any patentable innovation?
- Are patent records authentic and complete (number, title, date, inventors, filing country, link)?
- What is the candidate’s inventor role (lead/co/contributor)?
- Do contributions include applied invention beyond publications?
- Does the profile show research translation into practical/commercial outcomes?

- Topic Variability
- Is the candidate a specialist with deep work in one narrow domain?
- Is the candidate interdisciplinary across related/unrelated areas?
- Has the research focus remained stable over time or shifted significantly?
- What are major themes, concentration percentages, dominant area, and diversity score?

- Co-Author / Collaboration
- Does the candidate frequently collaborate with the same research group (stable network)?
- Does the candidate collaborate with a wide range of authors (broader reach)?
- Is the candidate leading collaborations or mostly contributing within groups?
- Are there strong supervision-related collaborations with students?
- Is collaboration cross-institutional or interdisciplinary?
- What are key indicators (unique co-authors, recurring collaborators, network size, diversity)?

- Professional Experience
- Is the career timeline internally consistent?
- Are there overlaps between education and employment, and are they reasonable?
- Are there overlaps between multiple jobs, and are they legitimate?
- Are there unexplained employment gaps?
- Are gaps justified by productive activities (education/research/freelancing/etc.)?
- Does the profile show career continuity, progression, and professional maturity?

- Skills Alignment
- Are the candidate’s claimed skills credible?
- Are skills supported by actual work experience and responsibilities?
- Are skills supported by publication topics/research outputs?
- Are skills consistent across education, work, research, projects, certifications?
- How relevant are evidenced skills to the target job description?
- Which skills are strongly evidenced, partially evidenced, weakly evidenced, or unsupported?
- Which skills are central strengths, and which appear overstated?

- Missing Information / Clarification
- Which profile elements are missing, incomplete, or unclear (scores, dates, publication details, authorship)?
- What specific missing items should be requested from each candidate?
- Can personalized draft emails be generated per candidate for missing details?

- Cross-Field Overlapping Questions
- Do publication themes support claimed skills and stated specialization?
- Do educational gaps align with and get justified by experience timeline?
- Are high-impact publications consistent with authorship role and career stage?
- Do supervision claims align with co-author patterns and student-linked publications?
- Are skill claims evidenced jointly by job history plus publication record?
- Is the overall profile internally consistent across education, research, skills, and experience?
- Are weak/unsupported claims concentrated in any one section or spread across sections?
- Is candidate strength deep-specialist or broad-interdisciplinary when combining topic, skill, and role history?
- Do ranking/quality indicators (institutions, venues) align with demonstrated outputs and progression?
- What confidence level should be assigned to final candidate evaluation given verification completeness?
